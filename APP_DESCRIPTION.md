# NChat — Connect with Friends

**NChat** is a modern, fast, and secure messaging app that brings everyone you care about closer — one-on-one or in groups, by text, voice, or video. Built for speed and simplicity, NChat gives you everything you expect from a great chat app, wrapped in a clean, friendly design.

## ✨ Features

**💬 Messaging that feels instant**
Send texts, photos, videos, voice notes, and documents in private chats or group conversations. Messages appear instantly, even show up while you're offline and sync the moment you're back online, so you're never left wondering if it went through.

**😊 React, Reply & Forward**
React to any message with emojis, reply directly to a specific message so context is never lost, forward messages to friends or groups in a couple of taps, and edit or delete messages after sending.

**👥 Group Chats**
Create groups for your family, friends, or teams. Share media, see who's in the group, manage members, and keep every conversation organized in one place.

**📞 Voice & Video Calls**
Call your friends and groups with crystal-clear voice and video, right from the app. See your full call history — incoming, outgoing, and missed — so you never lose track of who called.

**📸 Status & Stories**
Share text, photo, and video updates that your friends can view for a limited time. See who viewed your status, and reply directly to anyone's story.

**🤝 Friends & Discover**
Send and manage friend requests, discover new people to connect with, and see who's online or when they were last active.

**🔔 Notifications That Keep You in the Loop**
Get notified the moment a message, call, or friend request comes in — with typing indicators and read receipts so conversations feel natural and alive.

**🔒 Privacy You Control**
Fine-tune your privacy settings, block people you don't want to hear from, and use disappearing messages for conversations that don't need to stick around forever.

**🎨 A Clean, Modern Experience**
A beautifully simple interface that's easy to navigate, fast to use, and designed to just get out of your way so you can focus on the conversation.

---

NChat is built to be a complete, reliable alternative to the messaging apps you already use — fast, private, and always evolving with new features.
